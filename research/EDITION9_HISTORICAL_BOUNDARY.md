# Edition 9 — Historical boundary review

**Edition date:** 29 October 2026. Original 3-video transcript collection and all previous audits remain preserved.

**Method:** independent publication and archival provenance; facts in a document are not automatically true; negative archive searches are bounded. Historical-source status and extraordinary claims are separately recorded.

## E9-01 — Australian memorandum / Bahnson CIA-cutout allegation

**Status:** documented archival paper + documented physics history; claimed intelligence link not corroborated

The signed Australian JIO memorandum (NAA A13693/3092/2/000, May 1971) is genuine. Kaiser and Rickles document Bahnson financing real general-relativity research, the Wheeler/UNC firewall, and independent 1958 electrostatic evaluations. These do not corroborate the separate alleged CIA cutout operation.

**Evidence:** 2018 archival study with citations to Wheeler memo 28 Nov 1955 (BDW), Palmatier 13 Aug 1958 (CDWM), and the original 1971 Australian document.

**Boundary:** No contemporaneous primary CIA/Australian file has been located naming Bahnson as an intermediary in the specific arrangement alleged by the interview. Do not infer CIA relationship from a published paper about university physics.

**Necessary original:** Item-level CIA/OSS/AFOSI or Australian archival file explicitly connecting the named Bahnson with the specific operation and dates, plus exact interview audio passage resolving antecedents.

**Links:**
- David Kaiser and Dean Rickles, The Price of Gravity (2018) — https://web.mit.edu/dikaiser/www/HSNS4803_03_Kaiser.pdf
- JIO Scientific and Intelligence Aspects of the UFO Problem, 1971, scanned file — https://documents.theblackvault.com/documents/ufos/australia/A13693_3092-2-000_30030606.pdf

## E9-02 — Medieval edict / Magonia sky-ship narrative

**Status:** original weather-magic decree and separate medieval critical account identified; aerial reporting law absent

Admonitio generalis (789) c.65 prohibits tempestarii, spells and augury. Agobard De grandine et tonitruis, surviving BnF Latin 2853, describes and disputes Magonia cloud-ship accusations; it is not a reporting mandate.

**Evidence:** Ninth-century manuscript and scholarly critical Latin editions already cited in Edition 8, together with separate early-modern Comte de Gabalis annotation genealogy.

**Boundary:** No independently attested Carolingian law demanding reports about alien visitors has been located; absence from these identified texts does not demonstrate impossibility of an unrelated lost manuscript.

**Necessary original:** Produce a dateable medieval manuscript witness or critical-edition citation with the exact alleged reporting-language, distinct from both texts.

**Links:**
- Admonitio generalis (789) c.65 critical Latin transcription — https://lacabalesta.it/biblioteca/MGH/Capitularia/Capitulare022_AdmonitioGeneralis.html
- Agobard De grandine et tonitruis II: Magonia ships — https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Liber_de_grandine_et_tonitruis

## E9-03 — Puharich entire Tesla / TMT manuscript

**Status:** contemporaneous self-citation + family publishing account + first-person copy-holder corroborated as records; full manuscript not accessed

Puharich cites TMT, The Magnetic Threat in his 1984 paper as unpublished under contract with Dell. A family memoir describes a 1978 submission to editor Christopher Kuppig, but does not prove the alleged interference. Tesla historian Marc Seifer (2022) says he possesses a copy and describes a longer manuscript title and topics. RT17 advertises a 49.53-MB scan.

**Evidence:** Dated 1984 authorial bibliography, memoir publishing narrative and named living copy-holder; exact online scan endpoint https://www.rt17.hr/download/11490/.

**Boundary:** The RT17 binary download could not be retrieved in this environment; Seifer copy, publisher contract, and a title-page comparison remain uninspected. Manuscript arguments are not established physical facts.

**Necessary original:** Obtain complete PDF or physical copy from RT17 or the identified holder; log SHA256, page count, title page and publication provenance; compare with cited anthology and the 1984 self-citation.

McCoy correspondence cites RAE AERO 1703 and unverified aircraft intelligence. ## E9-05 — Vatican / Mussolini Magenta transfer papers

**Status:** claimant provenance, internal date contradiction and Vatican institutional position documented; dossier unauthenticated

Roberto Pinotti describes anonymous batches received from 1996 and gives conflicting incident dates (April 11 and June 13 1933) within one text. The 2024 Catholic News Service interviews Vatican archive official Marco Grilli, who says relevant extraterrestrial documents are not held in Vatican Apostolic Archive.

**Evidence:** Pinotti’s own reproduced first-person narrative and separately quoted official statement, along with known anonymous custody break and a reportedly lost 30-page packet.

**Boundary:** Neither an accessioned Vatican holding nor independent 1933 contemporary reporting of a recovered non-human craft was located. The Vatican official statement cannot adjudicate hypothetical undiscovered material, while Pinotti’s claim cannot authenticate itself.

**Necessary original:** Original 1933 item with chain of custody and forensic record; item-level Italian / Vatican / US archives shelfmark and independent date reconciliation (April 11 vs June 13).

**Links:**
- Roberto Pinotti, The UFO Files of Mussolini — https://ufopedia.fyi/raw/articles/blackvault-mussolini-ufo-files-pinotti/
- Catholic News Service: Vatican archive official response, June 2024 — https://www.usccb.org/news/2024/angels-or-aliens-some-researchers-say-vatican-archives-hold-ufo-secrets

## E9-06 — William Stephenson to Josephine Brown telegram

**Status:** Stephenson intelligence role and later distinct archival records verified; alleged telegram not identified

CIA public history verifies Stephenson headed wartime British Security Coordination. Canada Declassified gives R219/RG25 Box 13 dossier 3-12-3 Part 1 for different 1955–56 Stephenson report correspondence. Neither matches the alleged wartime telegram to Josephine Brown. Beware other archivally documented people called William Stephenson.

**Evidence:** CIA official institutional historical biography and exact Canadian accession of a distinct BSC document; the alleged telegram persists in later family/interview recollections only.

**Boundary:** No dated original Stephenson–Josephine message, contemporaneous transmitted-text copy, envelope, or accession matching sender-recipient-date. A documented 1942 Navy separation telegram to another recipient is a different item.

**Necessary original:** Retrieve actual telegram scan or contemporaneous incoming/outgoing message log naming sender, recipient and date and corroborate the proposed 1944/45 mission separately.
